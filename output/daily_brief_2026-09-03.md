### Project 3: Portfolio Autopsy Engine
**One-line pitch:** Discover what's actually driving your returns — factor beta or real alpha — before an allocator does it for you.

**Problem it solves:** Most retail and emerging PM portfolios are unknowingly 90% factor exposure. This engine runs Fama-French 5-factor and Brinson-Hood-Beebower attribution on your paper portfolio, isolates residual alpha, adds FX overlay decomposition, and uses Claude to draft a monthly PM commentary explaining what you learned.

**Stack:** Python (pandas, numpy, statsmodels) · Ken French Data Library (free) · Claude Fable 5.1 · broker or manual data

**Build time:** 2–3 weeks to portfolio-ready

**Who cares:** Allocators, fund-of-funds analysts, endowments — and every interviewer who asks "how do you think about risk-adjusted returns?"

**LinkedIn angle:** "I ran Fama-French attribution on my own trading and discovered I had no alpha — just hidden factor exposure. So I built a tool that finds it automatically."
