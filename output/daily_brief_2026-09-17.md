## 💡 Project Ideas

### Project 1: FX Regime Detector & Hedge Advisor
**One-line pitch:** An always-on risk agent that tells a trading desk when the macro regime has shifted and what their hedge book needs to look like right now.
**Problem it solves:** FX and crypto desks re-evaluate hedges reactively, after a regime shift has already cost them. There is no lightweight tool that continuously monitors macro signals (rates, volatility surfaces, correlation breaks), classifies the current regime, and produces a plain-English hedge recommendation with position-sizing rationale.
**Stack:** Python, yfinance + FRED API + Binance API; scikit-learn HMM or GMM for regime classification; Claude Sonnet 4.6 for narrative generation; Streamlit or single-page HTML dashboard; optional Supabase for logging regime transitions.
**Estimated build time:** 20–25 hrs to MVP / 40–50 hrs to portfolio-ready
**Who would care:** Risk managers and heads of trading at FX/crypto brokerages; multi-family offices running currency overlays; CFA/CAIA interviewers; VC investors evaluating AI-in-finance founders.
**LinkedIn post angle:** "I built a tool that classifies FX market regimes in real time and auto-drafts hedge recommendations. Here is what I learned about the gap between academic regime models and what actually works on a live book — and how I used an LLM to bridge the last mile from signal to decision."
**Difficulty:** MEDIUM

### Project 2: Investor-Grade Deal Memo Generator
**One-line pitch:** Feed it a company name and a funding round; it returns a four-page institutional-quality deal memo with comps, risk flags, and a recommended pass/proceed — in under 60 seconds.
**Problem it solves:** Junior analysts at VC firms and family offices spend 4–8 hours producing first-pass deal memos. An agentic pipeline that orchestrates web search, SEC/Crunchbase/LinkedIn data, and a finance-tuned LLM prompt chain can compress this to seconds, letting analysts focus on the judgment call.
**Stack:** Python orchestration; Claude Sonnet 4.6 with tool use/multi-step agent loop; Perplexity or Tavily API for live web search; SEC EDGAR API; Crunchbase Basic API; WeasyPrint or React/HTML for PDF output; optional Supabase to store memo history.
**Estimated build time:** 15–20 hrs to MVP / 35–45 hrs to portfolio-ready
**Who would care:** VC associates and partners; investment management recruiters; any fund reviewing more than 20 deals per week; your own future fund.
**LinkedIn post angle:** "I built an agentic deal memo tool that goes from company name to four-page institutional memo in under a minute. Walking through the architecture taught me more about what VCs actually look for in diligence than three years of reading about it."
**Difficulty:** MEDIUM

### Project 3: Real-Time Crypto Risk Dashboard with LLM Narrative Layer
**One-line pitch:** A live risk monitor for a crypto portfolio that not only shows Greeks and VaR but explains, in plain English, what the current risk picture means and what to do about it before market open.
**Problem it solves:** Crypto risk tools surface raw data but leave interpretation to the analyst. This tool automates the narrative layer, giving a risk officer or PM a 200-word situation report every morning without a single manual input.
**Stack:** Python + APScheduler; Binance + Deribit + Coinglass public APIs; pandas for risk calculations (delta, VaR, notional exposure); Claude Sonnet 4.6 for narrative briefing; Resend for email delivery; Supabase for logging daily snapshots.
**Estimated build time:** 12–18 hrs to MVP / 30–40 hrs to portfolio-ready
**Who would care:** Your current employer (direct on-the-job value, visa-relevant); crypto fund risk officers; trading desk heads evaluating AI tooling; CAIA and CFA examiners; fintech recruiters.
**LinkedIn post angle:** "I got tired of manually synthesising five data sources into a morning risk brief, so I built an agent that does it for me. Here is what surprised me about where the LLM adds value versus where the maths has to do the work."
**Difficulty:** EASY
