## 💡 Project Ideas

### Project 1: FX Regime Classifier with LLM-Augmented Macro Overlay

**One-line pitch:** An automated system that detects FX market regimes (trending, mean-reverting, volatile) and enriches the signal with a live macro narrative scraped from central bank communications.

**Problem it solves:** Discretionary FX traders and risk managers manually read Fed/RBA/ECB minutes, speeches, and meeting summaries to contextualize their positioning — a slow, inconsistent process that introduces latency into hedging decisions. This tool makes that synthesis instantaneous and auditable.

**Stack:**
- Python (pandas, scikit-learn, hmmlearn for Hidden Markov Model regime detection)
- Anthropic Claude API for summarizing central bank text and extracting hawkish/dovish stance scores
- FRED API or Alpha Vantage for FX rate data
- PyMuPDF or pdfplumber for ingesting PDF meeting minutes
- Streamlit or a simple HTML dashboard for output

**Build time:** MVP 20–30 hrs | Portfolio-ready 60–80 hrs | Maintenance 1–2 hrs/wk

**Who cares:** FX desk heads, risk managers, CAIA/CFA interviewers, macro fund recruiters (Winton, Man AHL, Millennium)

**LinkedIn angle:** "I built a tool that reads central bank minutes so I don't have to — here's what the Fed was actually signaling in 2023 vs what the market heard." Show regime states overlaid on EUR/USD price with hawkish/dovish scores below. Attach backtested Sharpe by regime.

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM

### Project 2: AI-Powered Portfolio Risk Monitor with Plain-English Alerts

**One-line pitch:** A lightweight risk dashboard that ingests a multi-asset portfolio, computes real-time Greeks/VaR/drawdown, and explains every breach in plain English — the risk report that junior analysts spend hours writing, delivered in seconds.

**Problem it solves:** Risk reporting at most small funds and family offices is a Friday-afternoon Excel job. By the time a coherent narrative reaches the PM, the market has moved. This tool runs continuously, detects threshold breaches (VaR exceedance, correlation spikes, concentration risk), and generates an instant one-paragraph explanation of what happened and why it matters.

**Stack:**
- Python (numpy, scipy, yfinance or Polygon.io for pricing)
- PyPortfolioOpt or QuantStats for risk metrics
- Anthropic Claude API for generating breach narratives
- Scheduled via cron or GitHub Actions for daily runs
- Output: email digest via Resend or a hosted Streamlit dashboard

**Build time:** MVP 15–25 hrs | Portfolio-ready 50–70 hrs | Maintenance 1–2 hrs/wk

**Who cares:** Family office COOs and CFOs, hedge fund recruiters, angel investors/VCs evaluating AI-in-finance infrastructure

**LinkedIn angle:** "I automated the risk report that used to take my team 3 hours every Friday. Here's the system, and here's the AI-generated narrative it produced during the August 2024 carry unwind — compared to what was actually written at the time."

**Difficulty:** EASY-MEDIUM

### Project 3: Autonomous Earnings Intelligence Agent for Small-Cap Equity Research

**One-line pitch:** An agentic research assistant that, given a ticker, autonomously pulls the last four earnings transcripts, financial filings, and recent news, then produces a structured investment memo with bull/bear/base cases — the first draft an analyst would be proud to put their name on.

**Problem it solves:** Small-cap and emerging markets equities are chronically under-researched because sell-side coverage is thin. A fund launching with limited headcount cannot do deep fundamental research at scale. This agent does the 80% legwork — ingesting primary sources, extracting key metrics, flagging management credibility signals, and structuring a memo — so the human makes the judgment call.

**Stack:**
- Python with an agentic loop (lightweight custom or LangGraph)
- Anthropic Claude API with extended thinking for multi-step financial reasoning
- SEC EDGAR API (free) for 10-K/10-Q filings
- Polygon.io or Yahoo Finance for price and fundamental data
- Structured output via Pydantic to enforce memo schema
- PDF export via WeasyPrint or ReportLab

**Build time:** MVP 25–40 hrs | Portfolio-ready 80–100 hrs | Maintenance 3–5 hrs/wk

**Who cares:** VC firms evaluating AI research tooling, long/short equity fund PMs, CFA interviewers, potential co-founders

**LinkedIn angle:** "I built an agent that writes a first-draft equity research memo in under 4 minutes. I ran it on $NVDA in January 2024 — before the earnings. Here's what it flagged versus what the consensus missed."

**Difficulty:** HARD (but the one that gets you the meeting — it reads as a product, not a script)
