## 💡 Project Ideas

### 1. FX Regime Monitor `MEDIUM`
**Pitch:** An always-on macro intelligence layer that tells traders when the rules of the market have changed before their P&L does.

**Problem:** FX desks and risk managers react to regime shifts (risk-on/off, vol spikes, carry unwinds) after the fact. There is no lightweight tool that continuously classifies the current macro regime and surfaces actionable alerts without a Bloomberg terminal.

**Stack:** Python, Claude Sonnet (narrative summarisation), yfinance + FRED API (VIX, DXY, yield curve, commodity proxies), scikit-learn HMM or KMeans for regime classification, Streamlit dashboard, Resend for email alerts

**Build time:** 3–4 days to MVP

**Audience:** Hedge fund risk teams, prop trading desks, investment management recruiters, CFA/CAIA evaluators

**LinkedIn angle:** *"Built a live macro regime classifier using hidden Markov models on FX and rates data — then wired Claude to write the daily risk brief so a trader can read the market in 60 seconds. It caught the April 2025 vol spike two days before my desk did."*

---

### 2. Earnings Call Alpha Extractor `EASY`
**Pitch:** A research co-pilot that reads every earnings call transcript and tells you what management is actually saying versus what they want you to hear.

**Problem:** Fundamental analysts and portfolio managers spend hours reading earnings transcripts hunting for tone shifts, guidance hedges, and forward-looking risk language. LLMs can do this in seconds but no one has packaged it as a structured, comparable output across a watchlist.

**Stack:** Python, Claude Sonnet with extended thinking (sentiment + uncertainty extraction), SEC EDGAR full-text search API, structured JSON output schema, pandas for cross-quarter comparison, Streamlit or static HTML report

**Build time:** 2–3 days to MVP

**Audience:** Long/short equity PMs, VC associates, investment banking analysts, fundamental research / credit recruiters

**LinkedIn angle:** *"Fed Claude every BNPL and payments-sector earnings call for the past 8 quarters and built a structured sentiment diffusion index — management confidence is down 18 points YoY while revenue guidance held flat. That divergence is the trade."*

---

### 3. Agentic Portfolio Stress Tester `HARD`
**Pitch:** A fund manager's war-game simulator: drop any portfolio into it and an AI agent stress-tests it against historical crises and generates an investor-ready risk memo in minutes.

**Problem:** Emerging fund managers and allocators lack the quant infrastructure to run proper scenario analysis. Stress testing a multi-asset portfolio against GFC, COVID, taper tantrum, and custom macro shocks requires either Bloomberg or a quant team — neither of which a solo manager or small family office has.

**Stack:** Python, Claude Sonnet (memo generation + scenario narration), yfinance historical data, numpy/pandas for correlation and drawdown math, agentic loop that selects and applies relevant historical windows, optional Monte Carlo via scipy, PDF export via WeasyPrint or Jinja2 HTML

**Build time:** 5–7 days to full MVP

**Audience:** Family offices, emerging fund managers, allocators, CAIA/CFA interview panels, VC/PE firms doing co-investments

**LinkedIn angle:** *"Built an agentic stress tester that takes a CSV of holdings, replays 6 historical crises against the portfolio, and uses Claude to write the risk memo an allocator would actually read. Ran my own book through it — the 2022 rates shock would have hit harder than 2020 COVID. That insight changed my hedging approach."*
