### Project 1 — FX Regime Radar

**One-line pitch:** "An always-on macro regime detector that tells you whether the market is trending, ranging, or in crisis — before you put on a trade."

**Problem it solves:** FX and crypto desks waste hours each morning manually scanning charts and news to contextualize overnight moves. There is no lightweight, automated layer that classifies the current market regime (risk-on/risk-off, trending/mean-reverting, vol spike) and surfaces the key drivers in plain English.

**Stack:** OANDA or Alpaca FX API · CoinGecko · FRED (VIX, DXY, yield curve) · hmmlearn HMM · Claude Sonnet 4.6 for narrative · Streamlit

**Build time:** 3–5 days

**Who would care:** Afterprime FX desk · IM/macro fund recruiters · CAIA/CFA interviewers

### Project 3 — Live Hedging Assistant

**One-line pitch:** "A risk co-pilot that watches your FX/crypto book in real time, flags when your delta or VaR is drifting out of mandate, and suggests hedge trades in plain English."

**Problem it solves:** Trading operations teams at brokers monitor positions, currencies, and hedge ratios manually and reactively. An agentic layer that computes real-time risk metrics, detects threshold breaches, and generates natural-language explanations with candidate hedges — without requiring Bloomberg.

**Stack:** CSV/mock WebSocket position feed · pandas/numpy risk engine (delta, net exposure, 1-day historical-sim VaR) · APScheduler (5-min polling) · Claude Sonnet 4.6 for breach + hedge suggestion · Streamlit dashboard with live gauges · Resend/Slack webhook alerts

**Build time:** 7–10 days

**Who would care:** Afterprime directly (your job, productionized) · quant shop recruiters · solo PMs · CAIA L2 risk management section
