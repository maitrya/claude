### Project 1: HedgeScope

**Pitch:** An agentic FX exposure monitor that watches net positions across currency pairs and recommends delta-neutral hedges before client flow moves the book against you.

**Problem:** FX prime brokers accumulate net position risk from client flow across 20+ currency pairs simultaneously. Manual aggregation is slow — by the time a risk officer identifies the imbalance, the cost of hedging has moved. No tool unifies real-time net exposure, live forward curve pricing, and options skew into a single ranked hedge recommendation with rationale.

**Stack:** Python 3.12, OANDA v20 API, CCXT, Claude tool use (function calling), pandas, Streamlit, SQLite

**Build Time:** MVP: ~8 hours | Portfolio-ready: 5 days

**Audience:** FX prime brokers, risk managers at multi-strat funds, quant trading ops teams, hedge fund recruiters who know this problem from the inside

**LinkedIn Angle:** *"Built an agentic FX hedging tool in my own time — monitors net exposure across 20 currency pairs in real time and ranks hedge options by cost using live forward curves and options skew. A problem I solve manually at work every day; wanted to see how much of the aggregation layer an AI agent could own."*

**Difficulty:** `MEDIUM`

### Project 2: AlphaDD

**Pitch:** Drop in a hedge fund's pitch deck and DDQ — get back an institutional due diligence memo structured around the CAIA framework in 90 seconds.

**Problem:** Screening alternative fund opportunities means parsing 50–100 page PPMs and DDQs to surface 15 key risk factors — a 4–6 hour task per fund for a junior analyst. The bottleneck is extraction and structuring, not judgment; the judgment is already codified in CAIA's DD methodology, which makes it a tractable LLM problem with a clear credential to anchor it.

**Stack:** Claude 3.7 (PDF vision + structured output), PyMuPDF, SEC EDGAR API, ADV form parsing, 13F analysis, Jinja2 memo templates, FastAPI

**Build Time:** MVP: ~6 hours | Portfolio-ready: 3 days

**Audience:** Family offices, endowments, fund-of-fund allocators, boutique placement agents, hiring managers at alternatives shops

**LinkedIn Angle:** *"Used the CAIA L2 alternative investment DD framework as a blueprint and taught Claude to produce institutional-grade memos from raw fund documents. Upload a PPM and DDQ, cross-reference the manager's SEC ADV and latest 13F, and the memo comes out in under two minutes."*

**Difficulty:** `EASY`

### Project 3: Sentinel Brief

**Pitch:** A daily AI-composed risk narrative delivered to your inbox each morning — portfolio VaR, factor exposure drift, correlation regime breaks, and a forward macro calendar, without a Bloomberg subscription.

**Problem:** Independent fund managers and family offices need the same morning risk picture as a $10B fund: cross-asset VaR, factor exposure drift, regime indicators, and a macro event calendar. Bloomberg Terminal costs $25k/year. No open-source alternative synthesises all these signals into a plain-English brief that arrives before the market opens.

**Stack:** Python, yfinance, CCXT (crypto), FRED API (macro), Claude structured outputs, Resend (email delivery), Supabase + pg_cron (scheduling), pandas for risk calculations

**Build Time:** MVP: ~12 hours | Portfolio-ready: 10 days

**Audience:** Emerging fund managers, family office CIOs, CFA/CAIA candidates demonstrating applied quant skill, VCs evaluating fintech operators

**LinkedIn Angle:** *"Built a daily AI risk brief that emails me every morning before the open — portfolio VaR, Fama-French plus crypto factor exposures, correlation regime detection, and a macro calendar digest from 30 data sources. Runs on a $0.40/day stack. The Bloomberg morning brief, for people building toward running their own fund."*

**Difficulty:** `HARD`
