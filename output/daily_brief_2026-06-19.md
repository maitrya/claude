## 💡 Project Ideas

### Project 1 — FX & Crypto Tail-Risk Sentinel
**Pitch:** An always-on agentic monitor that surfaces regime-change signals across FX and crypto markets before they hit your P&L — the risk desk you can't afford to hire.

**Problem:** Trading ops professionals spend hours manually scanning Bloomberg, news feeds, and positioning data for early warning signals. By the time a tail risk is obvious, it's already in the price.

**Stack:** Claude Haiku 4.5, Python (`anthropic`, `pandas`, `requests`), Alpha Vantage + CoinGecko + FRED (all free), Resend/Slack webhooks, SQLite or Supabase

**Build time:** 3–5 days

**Who cares:** Afterprime (directly demonstrates value in current role), prop trading firms, crypto funds, risk managers at banks, employers hiring for systematic trading or quant risk roles

**LinkedIn angle:** "I built an AI risk sentinel that monitors 12 FX pairs and BTC/ETH 24/7 and sends me plain-English alerts when cross-asset correlation breaks down. Here's what it caught last week that I would have missed — and how I built it in a weekend using Claude Haiku and free market data APIs."

**Difficulty:** EASY

### Project 2 — Agentic Investment Research Analyst
**Pitch:** Drop in a ticker, get back a hedge-fund-quality one-pager in 90 seconds — earnings transcript analysis, 10-K risk flags, comparable valuation, and a bull/bear thesis, all cited.

**Problem:** Sell-side research takes analysts days; buy-side PMs need differentiated views in hours. Junior analysts spend 80% of their time on data gathering, not synthesis.

**Stack:** Claude Opus 4.8 with adaptive thinking, Files API for PDF ingestion, web search tool, structured outputs, Python (`anthropic`, `pdfplumber`), Streamlit frontend

**Build time:** 5–8 days

**Who cares:** Fund managers evaluating candidates, investment management firms hiring for research roles, VC firms assessing AI-in-finance founders, anyone interviewing for buy-side equity research

**LinkedIn angle:** "I fed Claude Opus 4.8 a 200-page 10-K, the last 4 earnings transcripts, and asked it to write me a one-pager with a buy/sell thesis and cited evidence. The output was indistinguishable from a junior analyst's first draft — and it took 90 seconds. Here's the prompt architecture and what I learned about where AI still needs a human in the loop."

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM

### Project 3 — Portfolio Factor Exposure Analyzer with AI Narrative Engine
**Pitch:** Upload a portfolio of positions and get back an institutional-grade risk attribution report — factor exposures, concentration flags, stress test scenarios, and plain-English commentary ready to send to an LP.

**Problem:** Small funds and family offices can't afford Bloomberg PORT or MSCI Barra. Excel-based risk decomposition is error-prone and doesn't communicate well to non-quant LPs.

**Stack:** Claude Sonnet 4.6 with code execution tool, Python sandbox (`pandas`, `numpy`, `scipy`, `matplotlib`, `yfinance`), structured outputs, `reportlab` for PDF export

**Build time:** 7–10 days

**Who cares:** Anyone running or aspiring to run a fund, institutional investors evaluating risk infrastructure, fintech employers building portfolio analytics tools, VC investors backing AI-in-finance companies

**LinkedIn angle:** "I built a tool that takes a spreadsheet of positions and produces an LP-ready risk report in under 2 minutes. Factor decomposition, concentration analysis, stress scenarios — all computed in a Claude sandbox — then a plain-English narrative written by the model. Here's why I built it, what it gets right, and the one thing it still can't do that a CFA candidate can."

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM
