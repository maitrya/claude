### Project 1: FX Regime Classifier + Risk Alert Agent

**One-line pitch:** An autonomous risk monitor that detects FX volatility regime shifts in real-time and pages a trader before the drawdown hits.

**Problem it solves:** FX desks and fund managers currently eyeball volatility — ATR, rolling vol, maybe a Bloomberg alert. There is no lightweight, deployable tool that classifies the *regime* (trending, mean-reverting, crisis) and fires a structured alert with a plain-English explanation. The 47% of hedge funds running gen-AI are doing it for equity research; FX risk monitoring is underserved and directly relevant to Afterprime's book.

**Stack:** Python (pandas, numpy, hmmlearn), Oanda/FRED API (free), Claude Sonnet 4.6, Resend/SMTP, Streamlit, Railway/Render free tier

**Build time:** 25–30 hours

**Who would care:** Afterprime risk/compliance team (directly deployable), quant fund recruiters, VCs evaluating AI-in-finance pitches

**LinkedIn post angle:** *"47% of large hedge funds are running gen-AI in production. Here's what the other 53% are missing — I built a regime-detection agent for FX in 3 days that explains *why* vol is spiking, not just that it is."*

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM

### Project 2: SEC AI-Washing Audit Tool for Fund Marketing Decks

**One-line pitch:** Upload a fund's marketing PDF — get back a structured risk report flagging every claim that could trigger an SEC "AI washing" enforcement action.

**Problem it solves:** The SEC is actively targeting funds that claim AI-driven alpha without substantiating it. Compliance teams and fund marketers have no fast, cheap way to pressure-test a deck before it goes to LPs. This tool reads the PDF, extracts every AI-related claim, cross-references it against SEC guidance, and outputs a colour-coded risk register.

**Stack:** Python + pdfplumber/pymupdf, Claude Fable 5 or Sonnet 4.6, Pydantic structured output, Streamlit drag-and-drop UI + Excel export

**Build time:** 15–20 hours

**Who would care:** Compliance officers at fund managers and RIAs, legal/compliance recruiters, CAIA/CFA exam networks, VCs evaluating RegTech

**LinkedIn post angle:** *"The SEC is going after AI washing in fund marketing. I built a tool that audits a PDF deck in 60 seconds and flags every claim that could trigger enforcement. Ran it on 5 real fund decks — you'd be surprised what comes up."*

**Difficulty:** EASY-MEDIUM

### Project 3: Agentic Equity Research Synthesiser with Source Transparency Scoring

**One-line pitch:** A research agent that pulls earnings calls, SEC filings, and analyst reports for any ticker, synthesises an investment memo, and scores each claim by source quality — so a fund manager can trust it.

**Problem it solves:** The #1 complaint with gen-AI research synthesis is hallucination and source opacity. Analysts don't trust output they can't audit. This tool shows *exactly* which sentence came from which source and scores source reliability (10-K > earnings call > analyst note > news article). The transparency layer is the differentiator.

**Stack:** Python agentic loop (no LangChain), SEC EDGAR API (free), Claude Sonnet 4.6 with citations mode, Pydantic memo schema, Streamlit/HTML export, yfinance

**Build time:** 40–50 hours

**Who would care:** Buy-side analysts and PMs at long/short equity funds, investment management recruiters, VCs/family offices evaluating AI research tools, Afterprime if they have prop/advisory activity

**LinkedIn post angle:** *"Andrej Karpathy just joined Anthropic. 47% of hedge funds are running gen-AI in production. But the #1 thing analysts still don't trust is AI research they can't audit. I built a research agent that shows you exactly which sentence came from which filing."*
