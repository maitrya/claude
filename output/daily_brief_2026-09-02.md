## 💡 Project Ideas — 3 Buildable Projects

### Project 1: **FX Sentiment Radar**
**Pitch:** "I built a live FX sentiment engine that reads news, earnings calls, and central bank statements to flag regime changes before they hit the spot rate."

**Problem:** FX trading desks react to sentiment shifts hours after they're visible in text data. Manual monitoring doesn't scale.

**Stack:** Python · Claude Haiku 4.5 (cheap, fast summarisation) · NewsAPI or GDELT (free tier) · SQLite/Supabase for logging · Streamlit dashboard

**Build time:** ~12h MVP · ~25h portfolio-ready

**Who cares:** FX prop desks, Afterprime management, quant recruiters, any PM running macro books

**LinkedIn angle:** "I built a tool that reads 200 news articles per day and surfaces the 3 that actually matter to AUD/USD — here's what I found this week."

**Difficulty:** `EASY`

### Project 2: **Multi-Agent Investment Research Assistant**
**Pitch:** "I built a multi-agent AI system that decomposes an investment thesis into sub-questions, searches simultaneously, and synthesises a structured memo — in 90 seconds."

**Problem:** Investment research memos take analysts 2-3 days. Fragmented tasks (news, filings, comps, risk) map perfectly to agent specialisation.

**Stack:** Python · Claude Sonnet 4.6 (orchestrator) + Haiku 4.5 (sub-agents) · Financial data via Yahoo Finance/Polygon free tier · Tavily for web search · Markdown output

**Build time:** ~20h MVP · ~40h portfolio-ready

**Who cares:** VC associates, buy-side analysts, investment management recruiters, fund managers evaluating AI tools

**LinkedIn angle:** "I gave my AI the same brief as a junior analyst — here's how it performed vs. a real memo."

**Difficulty:** `MEDIUM`

---

### Project 3: **Natural-Language Backtest Builder**
**Pitch:** "I built a tool where you describe a trading strategy in plain English — 'buy when RSI < 30 and VIX spikes' — and it writes, runs, and reports a backtest in under 60 seconds."

**Problem:** Quant strategy ideation is bottlenecked by code. Traders have good intuitions they can't test. This bridges the gap.

**Stack:** Python · Claude Sonnet 4.6 (NL → code) · Backtrader or bt library · yfinance for data · Streamlit for UI · Supabase for saved strategies

**Build time:** ~30h MVP · ~60h portfolio-ready

**Who cares:** Quant recruiters, systematic hedge funds, algo trading platforms, Afterprime's execution desk

**LinkedIn angle:** "I turned 12 trading ideas from trader intuition into backtested strategies — 4 of them beat buy-and-hold. Here's the tool."

**Difficulty:** `MEDIUM`
